Local Library Reopens After Renovation

New reading rooms and extended opening hours welcome residents

By Jane Smith

The town library has reopened its doors after a six-month renovation, welcoming visitors back to a brighter and more spacious building.

The project added two new reading rooms and a children's area, and the library will now stay open until 8 p.m. on weekdays.
